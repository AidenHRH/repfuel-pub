# Welcome setup — design (short)

Owner request (2026-10-23): someone new downloads RepFuel and puts in their own details first (age, height, goal…),
so a shared copy never starts with the owner's numbers. Targets must be achievable, not far-fetched; BMI from age,
height and weight.

- First open of a new install: Welcome (age, height, weight, optional body fat, sex for the calorie estimate with
  "Prefer not to say") › Your goal (how active: 4 levels; Lose fat / Stay where I am / Build muscle) › Your
  targets (BMI with its NHS band and a "rough guide" caveat, estimated daily burn, suggested calories and protein,
  editable) › Start using RepFuel. The tour follows.
- Targets (Mifflin-St Jeor × activity): losing is at most 500 kcal and 20% under the burn (about 0.5 kg a week),
  never under resting energy or 1,200/1,500 kcal; building is +250 kcal; underweight and asking to lose keeps
  maintenance and says why. Protein 1.6 g/kg (maintain) or 1.8 g/kg (lose, build), using a healthy-BMI weight
  when BMI is over 25. Rounded to 50 kcal and 5 g.
- Phones with logs, workouts or meals from before 0.20 skip it (migration 018).
- Calls made without asking: sex and activity are asked (the estimate needs them); answers are kept in
  app_settings, not the profile table.
